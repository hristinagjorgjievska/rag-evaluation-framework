Nearest-Neighbor Classifiers

Requires three things
– The set of labeled records
– Distance metric to compute 
distance between records
– The value of k, the number of 
nearest neighbors to retrieve

To classify an unknown record:
– Compute distance to other 
training records
– Identify k nearest neighbors 
– Use class labels of nearest 
neighbors to determine the 
class label of unknown record 
(e.g., by taking majority vote)



Handling irrelevant and redundant attributes

measure

Kd trees
Separate by median or middle point of the range with respect to one attribute, x or y. The 
spliting point can be chosen to belong to the left or right subtree. At the next step make the 
splitin with respect to the other atribute etc. There are different techniques for the spliting 
criterion, depending of the application of the kd tree ( example subinterval local vs global 
variance ratio when kd is used for outliers detection, etc. )
