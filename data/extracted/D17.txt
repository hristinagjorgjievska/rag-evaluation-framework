---------- страница 63 ----------
Feature Normalization
•
Features with large magnitudes or ranges can dominate 
smaller-scale features, leading to biased learning.
•
Example: If one feature ranges between 0 and 1, and another 
ranges between 0 and 1000, the second feature will have a 
disproportionate impact on the model.
•
Normalizing features ensures gradients remain well-scaled, 
improving optimization efficiency.
•
In deep networks, unnormalized features can exacerbate 
issues like exploding or vanishing gradients, making 
training unstable.
•
Enables Faster Convergence in Neural Networks
